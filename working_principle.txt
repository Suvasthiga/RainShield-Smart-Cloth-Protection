RAINSHIELD - WORKING PRINCIPLE

1. The rain sensor continuously detects rainfall.
2. The ESP32 reads and processes the rain sensor input.
3. When rain is detected, Servo 1 moves the cloth/roof mechanism to the protected position.
4. The water-level sensor monitors the amount of water collected in the storage tank.
5. Servo 2 controls the water-flow mechanism.
6. When the tank is not full, collected rainwater is allowed to flow into the storage tank.
7. When the tank reaches the configured full level, water flow is stopped.
8. When rain stops, the cloth protection mechanism returns to its normal position.
9. The ESP32 continuously monitors the sensors and controls the actuators automatically.
